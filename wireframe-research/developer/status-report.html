6The real build (Next.js)

This turns the preview into a working system. Everything below is designed already; it needs a server, a database and real connections.

5AI connectionThrough the developer's server and key, counting tokens per client. Built to serve several clients from day one: the SaaS plan.

7Google & SEOEvery job as its own page, sitemap, "Google for Jobs" markup.
8Hosting, domain, backupsMove jaycvs.co.ke to the new site without losing Google ranking; daily backups.
9Data protectionPrivacy notice, consent on upload, automatic deletion after the retention period. Check whether Jay CVS must register with the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Suggested build order

  1. Public site + jobs board: quickest win; brings in traffic from Google and the WhatsApp channel.
  2. Orders, Paystack, tracking links and logins: where the money starts.
  3. AI: poster reading, CV tools, and the developer's token system.
  4. Team, Finance, alerts and the extras.

7AI and the developer's business model

The developer runs the AI on one account of their own and resells it to clients as token balances. Jay starts with 1,000,000 tokens, sees only tokens (never money), and asks the developer for more when they run low.

10-minute walkthrough

  1. A client chooses a package, uploads their CV, gets a private tracking link and pays.
  2. The order shows in red as "Not accepted"; a writer accepts it.
  3. The writer uses the AI assistant, uploads a draft; the client previews it and requests a change.
  4. Jay's dashboard: the money this month, the team, the token balance.
  5. Jobs inbox: drop 8 posters, see the scam and duplicate caught, approve, copy the WhatsApp channel post.
  6. Jay vs a writer: same order, two views. Only Jay sees money and team controls.
