      Keeping the fee safe

      • Shrink posters before sending. About 1000 px on the long side reads fine and keeps image tokens near the figures above. A full-size phone photo can cost 3–4 times more.
      • Set a monthly cap. The admin already has an AI budget setting (Website content → Business settings). In the real build, stop or switch to the cheap model when the cap is reached, and email yourself at 80%.
      • Mix models. Posters are simple to read: use the cheapest model that passes your accuracy test. Keep the stronger model for CV analysis, where quality sells.
      • Cache the instructions. The long instruction prompt is the same on every call; prompt caching makes the repeated part much cheaper on most providers.
      • Don't use free tiers for client CVs. Free tiers may use the data to train models. CVs hold personal details, so use a paid key.
      • Put the AI clause in the contract. e.g. "Fee covers up to N posters and M CV orders a month; above that, KES X per extra 100 posters."
