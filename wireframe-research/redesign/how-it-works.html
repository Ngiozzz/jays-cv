Questions about the process

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp

Do I need to visit your office?

No. Everything can be done on WhatsApp, phone and email. You're welcome to visit us in Nairobi if you'd rather meet in person.

Can I get my CV faster?

Yes. Professional and Executive include priority delivery within 24 hours. For an urgent deadline, tell us when you order and we'll do our best.

What if I don't like the draft?

Every package includes revisions. Tell us what to change and your writer will update it until you're happy.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. Your details are only used to write your documents. We never share them, and we only show a CV as a sample with your written permission and personal details removed.

Can you help if I'm applying abroad?

Yes. We write CVs for the UK, Canada, the US, the Gulf and Europe, following each region's format.
