Why Jay CVS exists

Most CVs we receive aren't weak because the person is weak. They're weak because nobody told them what a recruiter reads first, or that "responsible for duties as assigned" tells an employer nothing.

Jay started Jay CVS in 2018 to fix that. Every CV is rewritten around what the person achieved, in a format both recruiters and screening software can read, and for the jobs they're actually applying to.

The best CVs don't list everything you've done. They show what you can do for this employer.

The jobs board came next. Clients kept asking where to apply, so the team started checking and posting vacancies every morning. It's now how many people find us: they find a job, then make sure their CV is ready for it.

Today a small team of writers works on every order, and every Executive CV is still written by Jay himself.
