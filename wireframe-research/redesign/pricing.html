One payment by M-Pesa, straight to Jay CVS, when you upload your CV.

  2. Tap Pay with M-Pesa and enter your number
  3. A prompt appears on your phone. Enter your PIN

Do I pay before or after the work?

Before. Payment goes straight to Jay CVS by M-Pesa prompt or Pay Bill with your order number, and your writer starts as soon as it's confirmed.

Yes. Pay the difference and we'll add the extra services to your order.

Do you offer student or group discounts?

If we haven't started work, we refund you in full. Once a draft is delivered, we keep revising it until you're satisfied.
