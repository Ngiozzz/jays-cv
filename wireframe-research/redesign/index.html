How long does it take to get my CV?

Your first draft arrives within 48 hours of payment. Professional and Executive packages get priority, within 24 hours.

How do I pay?

After uploading your CV you'll see a Pay button. Choose M-Pesa and a prompt appears on your phone for your PIN, or pay by card. Payment is confirmed automatically and your writer starts right away.

What if I don't like the draft?

Every package includes revisions. Tell us what to change and your writer will update it until you're happy.

Do you write CVs for jobs abroad?

Yes. We write CVs for the UK, Canada, the Gulf and Europe, following each region's format.

I don't have a CV at all. Can you still help?

Yes. We'll send you a short questionnaire and build your CV from scratch.
