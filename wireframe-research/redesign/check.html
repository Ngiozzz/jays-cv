A fair fit, with clear gaps

You have the support and networking experience this job needs, but your CV hides the warehouse and CCTV work it asks for.

Wanjiru_Kamau_CV.pdf · checked 18 Nov, 14:32

What already fits

  • BSc in Information Technology, as the advert asks
  • Six years in IT support, above the five required
  • Hands-on with routers, switches and LAN set-up
  • Has trained staff on new systems

What the job asks for that your CV doesn't show

  • ?Any warehouse or multi-site work
  • ?CCTV or security systems
  • ?Supervising an IT team
  • ?Working to support SLAs

Words recruiters will search for

CCTVWMSMulti-siteUPSFirewallsAccess controlSLA

In the advert, missing from your CV. Use them only where they're true for you.

What to change first

  1. Add a line under your current job for the branches you support. If any are warehouses or stores, say so: "Support 3 sites, including the Ruiru warehouse."
  2. If you've set up or maintained CCTV, give it its own bullet. It's the second thing this advert lists.
  3. Put a number on your support work, like tickets per week or the response time you hit.
  4. Move your training of staff higher and call it what it was: "Trained 40 staff on the new ERP."
