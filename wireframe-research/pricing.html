What you pay, and how to pay

One upfront payment by M-Pesa, straight to Jay CVS. No hidden charges, and revisions are included.

Pay with M-Pesa

  1. Upload your CV and get your private tracking link (step 3 of the order card)
  2. Tap Pay with M-Pesa and enter your M-Pesa number
  3. A prompt pops up on your phone. Enter your PIN.
  4. That's it. Payment is confirmed automatically and your writer starts. No codes or SMS to forward.

Prefer the M-Pesa menu? Pay Bill 4027127 · Account number: your order number (e.g. JC-1048)
